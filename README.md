## ◆ About

I'm the **Founder & CEO of Alberoz Group**, the parent company behind **[Albero Technologies](https://albero.in)** — a SaaS and custom software studio in India that builds products businesses actually run on.

I came up through engineering (B.Tech in AI & ML), taught data science, and spent time in business development before founding the group. That mix shapes how we work: **every product starts with a business problem, not a tech stack.**
